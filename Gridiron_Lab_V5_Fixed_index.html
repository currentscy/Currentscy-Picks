Data quality matters

The app only generates projections when it has real team statistics for both teams. It does not invent player matchups, injury statuses, or 90% confidence levels. Results are experimental and have not been historically calibrated. Team scoring inputs are derived from completed 2026 ESPN NFL scoreboard games; turnovers are estimated because reliable turnover statistics are not included. NFL data availability depends on ESPN endpoints and browser permissions. No sportsbook odds are used.

Source: ESPN NFL. This is an independent prototype, not an official NFL product. Week is currently fixed to November 12–16, 2026; live NFL statistics and projections require network access.
